Streaming / incremental ASR extension trait.

Most voxora engines today are whole-audio-in / whole-text-out (see AsrEngine::transcribe). Some use cases — live transcription, voice activity detection, real-time UIs — need streaming: feed small audio chunks and get partial transcriptions back as they become available.

StreamingAsrEngine is the trait surface for that. It is additive for downstream callers: an Arc<dyn AsrEngine> keeps compiling. Engines opt in by implementing StreamingAsrEngine in addition to AsrEngine.
